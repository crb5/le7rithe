---
name: Rental season lifecycle
description: User-approved seasonal rental behavior and renewal fee boundary.
---

Rental listings begin with the season open to preserve current behavior. Closing the season archives active rental listings in place and must not delete listing data or photos. Reopening a season does not restore archived rentals automatically; owners renew them explicitly. Renewal fees start at zero, and a nonzero fee must not unlock renewal until payment is actually settled.

**Why:** The marketplace owner chose these release rules to avoid disrupting existing rentals while preventing closed-season publishing and unpaid future renewals.

**How to apply:** Preserve this lifecycle when changing rental search visibility, dashboard counts, admin season controls, or renewal/payment handling.
